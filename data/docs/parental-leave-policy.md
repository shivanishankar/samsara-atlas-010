# Parental Leave Policy

## Eligibility
- All full-time employees are eligible for parental leave regardless of
  tenure, for birth, adoption, or foster placement

## Duration
- Birthing parents: up to 16 weeks paid leave
- Non-birthing parents: up to 8 weeks paid leave

## Coordination with Other Leave
- Parental leave may run concurrently with any legally protected leave of
  absence; HR will coordinate the specifics with the employee
