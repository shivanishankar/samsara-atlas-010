# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Submission
- Submit expenses within 30 days of the expense date
- Include an itemized receipt for any expense over $25

## Travel
- Book travel through the approved travel platform when possible
- Reasonable meal expenses while traveling are reimbursable per the daily cap

## Disputes
- If a submitted expense is rejected, the employee can appeal to their
  manager and People Ops within 14 days of the rejection
