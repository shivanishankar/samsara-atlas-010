# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Samsarans accrue PTO based on level and tenure.

## Accrual
- PTO accrues monthly starting on day 1
- Unused PTO carries over up to 40 hours into the next calendar year
- Carryover balances above 40 hours are forfeited unless local law requires payout

## Requesting Time Off
- Submit requests through Workday at least 2 weeks in advance for planned time off
- Manager approval is required before time off is confirmed

## Payout on Departure
- Accrued, unused PTO is paid out on separation where required by state law
