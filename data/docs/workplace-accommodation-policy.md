# Workplace Accommodation Policy

## Requesting an Accommodation
- Employees may request a reasonable accommodation for a disability or
  medical condition through their HR partner at any time

## Interactive Process
- HR will engage in an interactive process with the employee to identify
  effective accommodations
- Medical documentation may be requested but is handled confidentially and
  stored separately from the employee's general personnel file

## Do Not Self-Determine
- Managers and HR partners should not independently decide whether an
  accommodation is reasonable without following the interactive process
