# Leave of Absence Policy

Covers medical leave, FMLA/CFRA-equivalent leave, and personal leave of absence.

## Eligibility
- Employees become eligible for protected leave after 12 months of employment
  and 1,250 hours worked in the prior 12 months (or per local law, whichever
  is more generous)

## Process
- Employees should notify their HR partner and manager as soon as the need
  for leave is known
- HR will provide required paperwork and confirm eligibility within 5
  business days

## Job Protection
- Eligible leave is job-protected per applicable federal, state, and local law
- Always route eligibility determinations through an HR partner - do not
  confirm eligibility informally
