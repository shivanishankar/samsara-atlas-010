# Harassment and Discrimination Reporting Policy

## How to Report
- Employees can report concerns to their manager, HR partner, or through the
  confidential ethics reporting line, including anonymously

## Investigation
- All reports are taken seriously and investigated promptly by trained
  personnel
- Confidentiality is maintained to the extent possible during an investigation

## Non-Retaliation
- Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith, or who participates
  in an investigation, is strictly prohibited and will result in discipline
